Setup types
A council asks several models, shows each the others' answers, and a chairman model gives the final probability. A single model answers alone. Baselines need no extra model: the mean and median of the council members' answers, and saying 0% to everything. The market is the Polymarket or Kalshi Yes price when the forecast was made.
